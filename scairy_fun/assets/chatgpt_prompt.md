# ScAIry Fun: title artwork prompt

Paste into ChatGPT (image generation) and save the result as
`assets/title_source.png`; `tools/make_title.py` (the converter Terra
Strike uses) turns it into a 32-colour, five-bitplane main screen.

> A 4:3 video game title screen in the style of a late-1980s / early-1990s
> Amiga game box cover, painted pixel-art look with a limited palette.
> A small red hatchback drives along a narrow road on top of a grassy
> levee at dusk; on the left a wide river, on the right a steep
> embankment dropping to dark fields. The car's headlights are on and a
> thin cyan line glows on the road ahead of it, like a self-driving car's
> planned path, but the line curves off the edge of the levee towards the
> water. Inside the car a nervous driver grips the wheel; above the car a
> friendly floating cyan holographic face is smiling far too confidently.
> Big chunky title lettering across the top reads "ScAIry Fun", with the
> letters "AI" glowing cyan and the rest in warm white with a dark outline.
> Leave the bottom fifth of the image as dark road and river so text can
> be drawn over it. No other text, no logos, no brand names.
